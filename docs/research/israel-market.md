# CVs in the Israeli tech market
Prefix: IL

How a CV moves through Israeli tech hiring in 2024-2026, from the candidate's side: the applicant
tracking systems (ATS) Israeli companies use, referrals ("חבר מביא חבר"), LinkedIn, job boards,
placement agencies and outsourcing firms, Hebrew vs English by employer type, and the 2024-2026
market only where it changes what goes on the CV. It does not repeat general CV advice. Cross
references: KB = `docs/cv-knowledge-base.md`, WP = `docs/cv-weak-points-research.md`,
SEC = `docs/cv-sections-research.md`. Reserve duty on the CV is covered by WP:3.3 and KB:E5;
referral disadvantage by WP:5.2; the junior market by WP:5.1.

### IL1 `ats-file-rules`: Send one PDF or DOCX under 6 MB, with contact details in the body
**Evidence: practitioner**

**Tip.** Upload a text-based PDF or DOCX, under 6 MB. Put name, phone and email in the page body,
because some forms do not collect them (see KB:B4, KB:B7).

**Why.**
- Comeet (Spark Hire Recruit) accepts resumes only as ".pdf, .doc, .docx", "Maximum size of 6MB" [Q1].
- Comeet, Tel Aviv-founded, "had served Israeli companies and customers for 10 years" before Spark Hire bought it in 2023; Fiverr and HiBob career pages run on it [Q4, Q5, Q6].
- Greenhouse hosts the boards of JFrog, Similarweb, Wiz, Cato Networks, Axonius, Forter, Melio, Payoneer and Riskified; its form takes a CV file or pasted text [Q7, Q8].

### IL2 `linkedin-can-be-the-application`: Keep the LinkedIn profile in line with the CV
**Evidence: practitioner**

**Tip.** Make titles, employers and dates on LinkedIn match the CV exactly, and keep the profile
complete. Some forms accept the profile instead of a file. See SR:linkedin-url.

**Why.**
- Comeet's form has a "require-profile" setting: "resume", "linkedin", "resume-linkedin" or "any" (either) [Q2].
- Comeet: "candidates can apply with their LinkedIn profile or upload a resume" [Q2].
- JFrog's Greenhouse form asks for "Resume/CV" and "LinkedIn Profile" side by side [Q8].
- Calcalist (2024), recruiter advice: make sure "קורות החיים שלכם והפרופיל המקצועי בלינקדאין מותאמים במדויק לתפקיד" (CV and LinkedIn profile match the role exactly) [Q28].

### IL3 `linkedin-keyword-variants`: Use the job's terms, and their variants, in the LinkedIn headline and experience
**Evidence: practitioner**

**Tip.** Repeat the target title and key skills in the LinkedIn headline and role descriptions,
including common variants (e.g. "Backend Engineer", "Server-side Developer"). See KB:B1, KB:B2.

**Why.**
- Gotfriends CEO (Geektime, 2026): recruiters search LinkedIn with "מילות מפתח חופשיות... ולא רק ברשימת Skills מובנית" (free-text keywords, not only the Skills list) [Q26].
- Same source: "כדאי להשתמש בווריאציות שונות של מילות מפתח" (use different variants of keywords) [Q26].
- SQLink AI head (Geektime, 2025): most recruiter outreach is now written with AI, so profile completeness matters [Q35].

### IL4 `referral-first`: Look for a referral before applying cold
**Evidence: survey**

**Tip.** Before applying through the site, check LinkedIn for a first- or second-degree contact at
the company and ask them to refer you. See WP:5.2 for who lacks such contacts.

**Why.**
- Viola survey of portfolio companies (2019): "90% ... had a friend referral recruitment program, and 47% named it as their most effective recruitment source" [Q9].
- AL Consultants survey of 300 startups (Mako, 2022): 56% of HR managers named "חבר מביא חבר" the best hiring method, against 13.2% for direct sourcing [Q10, Q11].
- Referral bonuses start at 5,000 shekels and reach almost 50,000; 5-10 thousand is typical [Q10].

### IL5 `referral-packet`: Give the referrer a ready file, the job link and two lines on fit
**Evidence: practitioner**

**Tip.** Send the referrer the CV as a PDF named with your name, the exact job link, and two or
three lines on why you fit. Do not also apply yourself to the same job.

**Why.**
- In Comeet's form, email and phone default to required "for candidates and `false` for referrals", so the file may be the only contact record [Q2].
- Comeet flags duplicates by email, phone, LinkedIn URL or name [Q3]; a second, direct application lands on the same record.
- Referral bonuses are paid per hire, so the referrer has a reason to submit it properly [Q10].

### IL6 `one-channel-per-company`: Reach each company through one channel
**Evidence: practitioner**

**Tip.** Pick one route per company: referral, agency or direct. Ask an agency which companies it
will send the CV to before agreeing.

**Why.**
- Comeet records who submitted a candidate, e.g. "the headhunter who submitted the candidate" (`source_contact`) [Q1].
- It detects duplicates across candidates and leads [Q3].
- Placement agencies are paid by the employer per hire [Q13], and placement firms also pay members of the public for referrals (Gotfriends: 2,400 shekels per hire) [Q12], so several parties may submit the same CV.

### IL7 `agencies-reformat`: Give agencies an editable DOCX and a Hebrew version if asked
**Evidence: practitioner**

**Tip.** Agencies are free for the candidate. Send them a DOCX as well as the PDF, check the
version they forward, and keep a Hebrew version for Hebrew-only agencies.

**Why.**
- Drushim: the agency "לא מעסיקה את העובדים" (does not employ the workers); its fee is agreed with the employer, not the worker [Q13].
- Agencies screen first and sometimes run the whole process; candidates often meet them only at late stages [Q13].
- CIVI, a Hebrew-first ATS for agencies and staffing firms, advertises CV analysis and PDF-to-Word conversion [Q14].
- Israeli agencies (SQLink, Gotfriends) take a CV file (doc, docx, pdf) or a LinkedIn profile [Q15, Q21].

### IL8 `outsourced-role-format`: For outsourced roles, name the employer and the client
**Evidence: practitioner**

**Tip.** Write the services company as the employer and the client under it, each with dates:
"Experis, placed at <client>, 2023-2025". If the client cannot be named, describe it ("a
tier-1 bank").

**Why.**
- About 50K Israeli high-tech workers are in "IT companies, providing additional services to the industry" [Q17].
- Experis Israel and SQLink sell "השמה טכנולוגית" and outsourcing, with consultants placed at client sites [Q15, Q16].
- Indeed: state "the staffing company that hired you and then name the individual companies you were engaged with" [Q18].
- Contract employment covers about 5-10% of Israeli workers, computing among the main sectors [Q19].

### IL9 `job-board-cv-is-public-ish`: Treat a job-board CV as seen by many employers
**Evidence: practitioner**

**Tip.** Upload to AllJobs or Drushim only a CV you are fine for any recruiter, including your
current employer, to see. Read the consent screens. For a quiet search, use one agency instead.

**Why.**
- AllJobs privacy policy: with specific consent it "עשויה להעביר למעסיקים ו/או לחברות מגייסות ... קורות חיים" (may pass CVs to employers or recruiting firms) [Q20].
- Drushim shares data for job search "לרבות ... משרות דומות ואחרות" (including similar and other jobs) and gives access to "מעסיקים פוטנציאליים שונים" [Q22].
- Gotfriends advertises getting job offers "בדיסקרטיות" (discreetly) [Q21].

### IL10 `forwarded-cv-stands-alone`: Make the file work when forwarded without a message
**Evidence: practitioner**

**Tip.** Name the file "Firstname-Lastname-Role.pdf", put the target title under the name, and
keep contact details in the body. Many CVs travel through groups and forwards. See KB:B3, KB:B4.

**Why.**
- A recruiter (Mako, 2022): "יש לי קבוצת ווטסאפ בשם קורות חיים בין חברות" (I have a WhatsApp group called "CVs between companies") [Q10].
- 3,000 people joined a Telegram group for junior jobs in one month (Globes, 2025) [Q23].
- Drushim recommends the LinkedIn group "Search & Find a Job in Israel" for job leads [Q24].
- Comeet gives each position its own email address for applying [Q2].

### IL11 `english-for-tech`: Default to English for tech companies
**Evidence: study**

**Tip.** Use an English CV for startups, Israeli tech companies and multinationals, even when the
ad is in Hebrew, unless it asks for Hebrew. See KB:H10.

**Why.**
- Israeli high-tech in Israel: about 90K in multinationals; private Israeli companies employ about 430K, only 190K of them in Israel; public ones 75% abroad [Q17].
- Ethosia (2026): Israeli tech workers abroad exceeded 440,000 [Q25].
- Drushim junior guide: "כתבו קורות חיים מקצועיים באנגלית" (write professional CVs in English) [Q27].
- Nefesh B'Nefesh: Hebrew is critical in some professions, "for others, such as hi-tech, it's less important" [Q29].

### IL12 `hebrew-for-defense-and-public`: Keep a Hebrew version for defense, public sector and local firms
**Evidence: practitioner**

**Tip.** Prepare a Hebrew CV for defense companies, government, municipalities and
Hebrew-only agencies. Do not state a clearance level; vetting is done by the employer.

**Why.**
- Israel Aerospace Industries' careers site is entirely in Hebrew and runs its own security-suitability questionnaire [Q30].
- Nefesh B'Nefesh: "If you are sending your resume to an Israeli employer, it's better to send a Hebrew language resume" [Q29].
- CIVI, built in Hebrew, lists a municipality and the Histadrut among clients [Q14].
- Defense-tech employs 34,400 professionals; software engineers are 28.3% of its openings (Ethosia, 2026) [Q31].

### IL13 `ai-use-with-result`: Name the AI tools you used and what changed
**Evidence: practitioner**

**Tip.** Add AI work as achievements: the tool, what you built or automated, and the measured
result. Building agents beats using chat tools. See KB:A1.

**Why.**
- Gotfriends CEO (2026): "ציינו כל ניסיון שצברתם בשימוש בכלי AI ... אם פיתחתם אייג'נטים ... הדגישו זאת" (list AI experience; stress it if you built agents) [Q26].
- SQLink recruiting head (2026): replace "development using GitHub Copilot" with a result such as Time-to-Market cut 40% [Q32].
- Microsoft-LinkedIn study cited by Calcalist: 66% of managers prefer candidates with AI skills [Q28].
- Innovation Authority survey: 10% of companies hired because of AI adoption in June 2026, up from 3% in December 2025 [Q33].

### IL14 `ai-role-specifics`: For AI roles, name models, the lifecycle stage and the production context
**Evidence: practitioner**

**Tip.** For ML or GenAI roles, name the models and methods (RAG, fine-tuning, LoRA), the stage you
owned (data, training, deployment, monitoring) and cost or latency results.

**Why.**
- Gotfriends and a data-science recruiter (Geektime, 2024): name the models, say whether work was internal, fine-tuning, or added to a product, and weave keywords into role descriptions [Q34].
- Same source: match your title to the company's naming; some call AI engineers "SW Engineers" [Q34].
- Gotfriends (2025): show RAG, fine-tuning/LoRA, latency work, MLOps, and cost awareness: "הרצת מודלים היא עסק יקר מאוד" [Q36].

### IL15 `defensible-lines`: Write only lines you can explain in an interview
**Evidence: practitioner**

**Tip.** AI can help draft, but every claim, number and tool on the CV must be one you can explain
in detail. See KB:A3.

**Why.**
- SQLink recruiting head (Geektime, 2026): recruiters spot AI-generated CVs and home tasks; when candidates cannot explain their own work, credibility collapses [Q32].
- Same source: if AI was used on a home task, attach the prompts, corrections and decisions [Q32].

### IL16 `layoff-one-line`: Say in a few words when a role ended in a closure or layoff
**Evidence: practitioner**

**Tip.** If the site closed or a layoff round ended the role, add a short note ("Israel site
closed, 2025"). Do not explain more on the CV. See WP:3.2 for gaps.

**Why.**
- Gotfriends CEO (2026): "אם הסייט כולו נסגר, ולא פוטרתם בגלל הביצועים שלכם – אפשר לציין זאת" (if the whole site closed and it was not performance, you can say so) [Q26].
- Innovation Authority (2026): 81% of tech companies reported layoffs in the first half of 2026; average layoff rate 2.8% [Q33].

### IL17 `junior-two-versions`: Juniors: widen to non-tech employers and IT services, with a version for each
**Evidence: survey**

**Tip.** Juniors should also target tech roles in large non-tech organisations, defense and IT
services firms, with a Hebrew or English version to match. See WP:5.1, KB:H6.

**Why.**
- Globes (2025): only 88 of 5,641 tech vacancies (1.5%) were for candidates without experience [Q23].
- Ethosia: job search for people with under two years' experience reached 20 weeks [Q25].
- Drushim: competition for tech jobs "קטנה משמעותית" (is much smaller) in large organisations [Q37].
- Experis says its retraining track placed over 2,000 people at client companies [Q16].

### IL18 `hebrew-ats-unknown`: Do not count on Hebrew parsing in global ATSs
**Evidence: convention**

**Tip.** For Greenhouse and other global ATSs, submit English. A Hebrew CV is safer with Israeli systems
and agencies, but no vendor document confirms Hebrew parsing quality.

**Why.**
- Comeet's Israeli selling point is support "based in Israel ... in Hebrew", not documented Hebrew parsing [Q4].
- CIVI markets itself as built in Hebrew for Hebrew-speaking recruiters [Q14].
- KB:H10 found no vendor test of Hebrew parsing; treat it as unknown.
